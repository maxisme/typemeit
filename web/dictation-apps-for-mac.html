how to read it

server says whether your audio leaves the mac. no means the speech model runs on the computer. optional means the app runs locally by default and can be pointed at a cloud model, usually with your own api key. yes means transcription happens on the vendor's servers.

account is whether you must sign in before dictating. not stated means the vendor's site does not say, which is not the same as no.

text appears is the difference between watching words stream in as you talk and getting the whole sentence when you release the key. neither is better. streaming feels faster; after-release lets the model hear the full sentence first.

prices are as published on the date at the top, in the vendor's currency. they change often. if a row is wrong, tell us and it will be fixed.

not on the list

aiko and modelpiper transcribe, but neither types into other apps from a hotkey, so they are a different tool. google docs voice typing works only inside google docs. dragon no longer ships for the mac.
