insertion studies · round 3

typewriter and terminal are both machines. typing has three other vocabularies that owe nothing to either: the marks an editor makes on paper, the cursor in a mac text field, and the conventions of a transcript. still lockup 01 — mark, one lobe of air, name — with one thing added.

the proof

pen on paper, older than any keyboard. an editor marking a page has a whole notation for "put words here", and it is the closest thing in print to what the app does. hand-drawn in origin, so it sits beside a hand-drawn puff.

3a

INSERT

the proofreader's caret, sitting under the line in the gap. it has meant exactly one thing for four hundred years — the text goes in here — which is the app's whole job. my pick of the nine.

the field

not a terminal — a mac text field, which is where the app actually pastes. the i-beam and the hairline insertion point are cocoa's, not a shell's, and they are the two shapes a mac user has looked at every day for twenty years.

the transcript

what dictation actually produces, and a vocabulary no other app on the mac gets to use. these three are the only ideas across all thirty that could not belong to a text editor, a shell or a typewriter.

3g

INTERIM

the tail the recogniser has not committed to yet. every dictation engine shows its provisional words faint and then firms them up; here the last word is still arriving. the most honest picture of the product on any sheet.

two directions i looked at and did not build, in case either sounds better than what is here: movable type — the name set as a letterpress sort, with the nick and the shoulder, which is "type" in the printer's sense rather than the typist's; and stenography — the curved strokes of shorthand, which would complement the puff beautifully and which nobody would be able to read.

my ranking of the nine: 3a insert, then 3g interim, then 3c quoted. 3a because the mark already means what the app does, 3g because no competitor can use it, 3c because it is the one i would still like in a year. everything is lockup 01 from design/logos.html with the puff from TypeMeIt/Support/Icon/puff.svg and tokens from design/tokens.json.
