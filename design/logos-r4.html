face trials · round 4

lockup 01 exactly as it is — mark, one lobe of air, name. nothing added, nothing moved. the only variable on this sheet is the typeface.

each name is set to the same x-height rather than the same point size, so what you are comparing is the drawing and not how large a face happens to set. the wordmark ships as outlined svg, so none of these costs a webfont at runtime — the site keeps dm mono for everything else either way.

the incumbent

what 01 is set in today, and the thing every plate below has to beat.

DM MONO

400 · −0.025em · the system face

even, quiet, and already everywhere in the app. its weakness as a wordmark is that it is a text face doing a display job — nothing in the drawing answers the puff's round caps, and the monospacing pushes "type me it" wider than the mark wants.

round terminals

the puff is drawn with round caps on every stroke. these three faces end their strokes the same way, so the name and the mark are made by the same hand. formally this is the closest match on the sheet, and where i would look first.

QUICKSAND

500 · −0.01em · geometric, round caps

circles and round ends, which is the puff's exact construction. set at 500 so it holds its own against the stroke weight — at 400 it goes wispy. warm without going soft.

other monos

if the app's voice should stay monospaced, these are the three worth considering over dm mono — each has a distinct character where dm mono is deliberately neutral.

SPACE MONO

400 · −0.02em · pairs with space grotesk

the most characterful mono on google fonts — slab-ish, slightly odd, memorable. it also has a sans sibling, so the wordmark and the ui could be one superfamily. the oddness is the whole point and the whole risk.

all thirteen are lockup 01 with the puff from TypeMeIt/Support/Icon/puff.svg at its own stroke weight; only font-family, weight and tracking change. x-heights are matched by eye rather than by metrics, so treat the sizes as close rather than exact.

my three: varela round and quicksand because round terminals are the one thing on this sheet that actually answers the mark's construction, and space grotesk because it is the only choice that would also reorganise the rest of the system into one family. if none of these land either, the next move is to stop shopping and draw the letters from the puff's own arcs.
