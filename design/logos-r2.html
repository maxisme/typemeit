typewriter studies · round 2

every plate here is lockup 01 — mark, one lobe of air, name on one line. the structure does not move. only the setting of the name changes, and only by the smallest amount that reads.

two halves. the machine plays the typewriter that the name already implies: typebar, platen, pica pitch, paper. the terminal plays the cursor the app actually types into. still one ink, one paper, dm mono, and the two emphasis moves.

the machine

a typewriter leaves evidence: the bar strikes twice, the platen rules the line, the pitch is fixed at ten to the inch, the sheet goes in crooked. each of these is one piece of that evidence and nothing else.

the typebar bounced and hit the paper twice, a quarter-point off. the word doubles rather than blurs — the impression, not a drop shadow. dies at 13 px, so it is a hero-only move with 01 underneath it everywhere else.

the terminal

the app pastes where the cursor is. these five put that cursor in the lockup — block, underscore, prompt, selection — so the mark says what the app does with one extra glyph and no new vocabulary.

1f
