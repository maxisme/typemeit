setting trials · round 5

dm mono throughout. no other face, nothing added. the letterforms are not touched — only how they are set: what they are spaced at, how heavy they are drawn, how they sit against the mark.

a subtle move cannot be judged from memory, so every plate carries the untouched setting directly beneath it. the grey line is today's 01. the black line is the change.

the fit

dm mono gives every glyph the same 0.6em slot, which is correct for code and wrong for a wordmark — it strands the i and the t in space the m has to fill. these three fix the spacing and leave the letters alone.

FITTED

t −0.07em · i −0.11em · y −0.03em

the narrow letters pulled into the rhythm of the wide ones, the way any wordmark is fitted rather than metric-set. nothing is redrawn. this is the one change here you would never notice and always feel.

the weight

the puff is drawn at a fixed stroke. the question is which of dm mono's three weights sits at that same colour on the page — at 400 the name is slightly heavier than the mark, which is why the lockup reads name-first today.

LIGHT

300

drops the name to roughly the puff's own stroke weight, so mark and name read as one drawing rather than a picture next to a label. the closest match on colour. gets fragile below about 15 px.

the fit to the mark

two adjustments that never touch the type at all — where the name sits relative to the puff. both are the kind of thing that is invisible until you see the pair.

CLOSE

gap 0.42em → 0.2em

the gap halved, so the name is held by the mark instead of placed beside it. today's 0.42em was one lobe wide; this is about half a lobe, and it makes the lockup read as one word with a picture at the front of it.

every line on this sheet is dm mono 400 unless the plate says otherwise, with the puff from TypeMeIt/Support/Icon/puff.svg at its own stroke weight. no letterform is redrawn: the fitting is per-glyph spacing, which survives being outlined into an svg exactly as shown.

the cut is my recommendation, and it is four small moves rather than one idea — fitted spacing, the word gaps matched to the mark gap, the mark held closer, the name dropped onto its optical centre. if any single one of the eight is the only one you want, fitted is the one that earns its place on its own.
